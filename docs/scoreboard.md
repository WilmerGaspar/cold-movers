**Very red fake movers (only the two longest-wavelength detectors), S/N 15.** Diagnosis: a detector is trusted only if its still stars line up between visits. At the longest wavelengths these fields hold only one to three stars. When one of them is a real mover (Epsilon Indi B or WISE 0855 themselves) or the halo of a saturated star (RW Aurigae), the rule distrusts the whole detector, so a second mover cannot be confirmed; in the richer TRAPPIST-1 field most are. Making this rule work in sparse fields is our first next step.
