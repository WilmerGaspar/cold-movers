# Scientific validation protocol and results

## Protocol

1. **Reference objects with known motion** (Barnard's Star, Epsilon Indi B, WISE 0855−0714, TRAPPIST-1):
   compare the motion measured in SPHEREx with the catalogue proper motion (SIMBAD / Gaia DR3).
2. **Blind detection** (`movers.search_all`): the search receives only images; it is compared with the catalogue
   afterwards. Uncertainty ≈ speed / significance.
3. **Catalogue-assisted trajectory** (`assistant.trajectory_fit`): positions measured within 2.5 px of the
   predicted track, weighted straight-line fit, 1σ errors from alignment (0.12 px) and centroid noise
   (0.9 px / S/N), errors scaled by √χ²ᵣ when χ²ᵣ > 1. Labelled as catalogue-assisted in the app.
4. **Catalogue confirmation**: a mover is matched to a Gaia DR3 star only if both position and proper motion agree.
5. **Control fields**: stars that do not move (R Draconis, RW Aurigae) must not produce a mover; no unexplained
   confirmed mover may appear in any field.
6. **Temperatures**: blackbody-equivalent temperature compared with published effective temperatures only where
   that comparison is meaningful (unsaturated, peak inside SPHEREx's range): TRAPPIST-1. Different quantities;
   the comparison is a consistency check.
7. **Refusals**: objects SPHEREx cannot measure (saturated, peak beyond 5 µm) must get a red light.
8. **Variability**: known variables (R Draconis, RW Aurigae) should show a change between visits.
9. **Injection**: fake movers in real images (scoreboard campaign and the blind benchmark, `BENCHMARK_RESULTS.md`).

The scoreboard (`docs/scoreboard.md`) applies pass rules fixed in `coldmovers/scoreboard.py` before its results
were known; every result is published, including partial and failed ones.

## Evidence records

`docs/validation_records.csv` and `docs/validation_records.md` are generated by `scripts/validation_report.py`
with the app's own code on GitHub Actions. For every object they record: the SPHEREx files used (one IRSA URL per
image in `data/precomputed/<object>/photometry.csv`, which also holds the observation date, detector, wavelength,
position and quality flags of each image), visits and dates, detectors, the blind motion with its uncertainty and
status, the trajectory fit with its uncertainty and χ²ᵣ, the catalogue motion, the difference in σ, the
blackbody-equivalent temperature with its range and traffic light, the published value, the brightness change,
the four consistency checks and the code version.

## What this validation does not show

* No discovery: every result is a test on known objects.
* The blind search is not validated in crowded fields beyond the benchmark's "near a star" spots.
* Photometric calibration against an independent survey (2MASS, WISE) was not done.
