# Calibration follow-up (exploratory, not pre-registered)

Written after seeing the pre-registered results in [CALIBRATION.md](CALIBRATION.md), to understand them. Generated by `scripts/calibration_followup.py`. Nothing in the app, in any decision or in any displayed number uses it.

## Blind-search speed uncertainties are too small

The pre-registered scale (RMS of the pulls) is driven by a tail of very large pulls. Its robust version, the median pull divided by the median of a Rayleigh distribution, is **1.67** on the development set (536 confirmed injections) and **2.25** on held-out A (350). The 90th percentile pull is 8.9 and 7.9. Reading: the quoted blind-search uncertainty (speed / significance) should be taken as **at least about 1.6 times larger**, with heavier tails than a Gaussian. The likely cause: the combined significance adds the detectors as if their errors were independent, but they share the same sky, neighbours and alignment.

## The temperature interval is quantized

The interval keeps the points of an 800-point grid (steps of 0.35 %) where Δχ² ≤ max(1, χ²r). It can therefore be up to one step too narrow on each side, and it is empty when the true interval is narrower than one step. That is why the precise synthetic spectra fail the coverage test. The best-fit temperatures themselves are not biased (12 synthetic spectra per temperature: 800 K: -0.2 ± 0.6 K; 1200 K: -0.7 ± 1.8 K; 1800 K: +1.6 ± 3.3 K; 2500 K: +1.6 ± 7.1 K; 3200 K: +0.0 ± 11.1 K). For the real objects the intervals are wide (their χ²r is large), so the effect is small:

| Object | Best fit | Reported interval | Exact scan | Reported too narrow by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TRAPPIST-1 | 2,464 K | 2,443–2,486 K | 2,441–2,488 K | 9 % |
| Epsilon Indi B | 1,874 K | 1,801–1,944 K | 1,795–1,949 K | 7 % |

The fix (find where the χ² profile crosses the level by interpolating between grid points) is not applied in this release, as pre-registered; it must be checked again on new synthetic spectra before it is used.
